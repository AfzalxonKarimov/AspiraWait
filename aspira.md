# Aspira

## What is Aspira?

**Aspira** is an AI mentor for ambitious students.

Students have big dreams — getting into their dream university, landing a meaningful
internship, building a portfolio that stands out. But they often feel lost: What
should I do next? Where can I find the right opportunities? Am I on track?

Aspira answers those questions with a personalized roadmap, curated opportunities,
and progress tracking — all delivered by an AI mentor that understands your goals.

## Who is it for?

- **Ambitious high school students** aiming for top universities
- **Undergraduates** looking to discover internships, scholarships, and extracurriculars
- **Students who want personalized guidance** that adapts to their unique profile and timeline

## Why is it different?

People don't buy features — they buy outcomes.

| Instead of...          | Aspira delivers...                                        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI Roadmap             | Never wonder what to do next again.                       |
| Opportunity Database   | Discover scholarships, hackathons, and internships that actually match your profile. |
| Progress Analytics     | Know whether you're getting closer to your dream university every week. |

## How can I join?

Join the waitlist on the landing page. We collect:

- **Email** (required)
- **Country** (required)
- **Age** (required)
- **Dream university / career** (optional)

These answers tell us who our early users are, so we can build the right thing.

## Validation strategy

A waitlist alone doesn't validate demand — anyone can type an email.

The real validation is willingness to spend time talking.

After someone joins, we send them a message offering a 15-minute call:
_"Thanks for joining! I'd love a 15-minute call to learn about your biggest
challenge in planning your future."_

If 20–30 people agree, we learn more than months of guessing.

## Tone

Friendly, aspirational, and honest.

- **Not** a polished "finished company" landing page
- **Is** something exciting that's coming soon
- Focuses on outcomes, not feature lists
